About

Your work shouldn't be
scattered across ten apps.

Tasks in one app. Files in another. Notes somewhere else. A calendar that doesn't know about any of them. Most people spend more time moving between tools than doing the work those tools are for.

mova starts from a different place: the thing you're working on. Open a project and its tasks, notes, files, deadlines, links and code are already there. Open the app and the first thing you see is what matters today.

The name comes from movement — from idea, to plan, to work, to finished. That's the whole philosophy: Think. Plan. Make.

What we believe

Less managing, more doing

No fifteen databases and forty tags. Few buttons, few settings, few decisions — then mova gets out of your way.

Dense when you need it, empty when you don't

Information-rich when you're planning; nothing but one task and a timer when you're focusing.

AI that helps, quietly

AI is a helper inside the system, not the product. mova works perfectly without it, and it never does your work for you.

Your data is yours

Your workspace lives on your device and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. No ads. No tracking.

Built on open source

mova is built with Tauri and React. Its code editor is Monaco, the open-source core of Code - OSS and Visual Studio Code. File icons come from the Material Icon Theme, and the typeface is Inter. Thank you to everyone who maintains them — see the open-source licences.
