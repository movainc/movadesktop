Features

Everything connected.
Nothing extra.

mova brings the important parts of your work into one place, organised around the projects you're actually working on.

TodayProjectsTasksCalendarNotesFiles & DriveCodeFocusSearchAIAccounts

Today

Know what matters right now.

  • Your events and scheduled tasks in time order, with a live "now" line
  • Tasks due today and anything overdue
  • A suggested focus for what to do next
  • Deadlines coming up this week, and everything you opened recently

Projects

Work on something, not in an app.

  • Areas for School, Work, Personal, Content and Side projects
  • Overview, Tasks, Notes, Files, Calendar and Links for every project
  • Choose a colour and an icon; star projects to pin them
  • Progress and deadlines at a glance

Tasks

Fast to add. Easy to finish.

  • Today, Upcoming, All, No project and Completed views
  • Quick add with a project and due date as you type
  • A detail panel to schedule, move or focus on a task

Calendar

Turn intentions into time.

  • Drag an unscheduled task onto the week to give it a time block
  • Drag blocks to move them; double-click to add an event
  • Deadlines and due tasks at the top of each day

Notes

Capture first. Organise later.

  • Plain, fast writing — no databases, no setup
  • Select a line and turn it into a task linked back to the note
  • Notes belong to projects and show up in search

Files & Google Drive

Your files, with your work.

  • Folders, Starred, Recent and Shared, with real file-type icons
  • Drag and drop to upload; attach files to projects
  • Desktop: browse Google Drive and open, edit and save files that live there

Code

A real editor, built on Code - OSS.

  • Powered by Monaco, the editor at the heart of VS Code
  • Highlighting for 80+ languages, tabs, find & replace, multi-cursor
  • Auto-save — to your workspace, or straight to Google Drive

Focus

One task. Nothing else.

  • 25, 50 or 90 minute sessions
  • Mark done and move straight to the next task
  • Everything unnecessary disappears

Search

Stop remembering where you put things.

  • One box across projects, tasks, notes, files and events
  • Search a project's name to see everything inside it
  • Quick actions and navigation from the keyboard

AI · desktop

Small, optional, and on your side.

An assistant that answers "what should I work on next?", suggestions of next steps from a note, and breaking a task into smaller ones. You review everything; nothing is added without you. It's designed to help you start — not to do your work for you.

Accounts

Sign in with Google.

Each account gets its own private workspace. On desktop, choose to keep it on this device only, or also sync it to a private folder in your Google Drive so it follows you between computers.
