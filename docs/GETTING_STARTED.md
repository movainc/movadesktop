# Getting started

## 1. Get mova

- **Desktop:** download the installer for your computer from [movadesktop.vercel.app/download](https://movadesktop.vercel.app/download) and install it.
- **Web:** open [movadesktopweb.vercel.app](https://movadesktopweb.vercel.app) — nothing to install.

## 2. Sign in with Google

mova asks you to sign in before you use it, so your workspace is private to your account.

![The sign-in screen](screenshots/signin.png)

- **Desktop:** press **Continue with Google**. Your browser opens so you can pick your account; when it says *You're signed in to mova*, switch back to the app. Choose where your workspace is saved:
  - **This device + Google Drive** — saved on this computer and synced to a private mova folder in your Drive, so it follows you to other computers.
  - **This device only** — nothing leaves your computer.
- **Web:** use the **Sign in with Google** button. Your workspace is saved in that browser.

> The first time mova runs on a computer, the sign-in screen may ask you to **set up Google sign-in**. That's a one-time step for whoever builds or installs mova — see [Google sign-in](AUTH.md).

## 3. Take the tour

A new account starts with an empty workspace and a short, hands-on tour. It asks you to actually do each step, and moves on when you have:

![The tour guiding you to create a project](screenshots/tour-step.png)

1. **Create a project** — pick a name, area, colour and icon.
2. **Add a task** in that project.
3. **Give it a time** — drag it onto the calendar.
4. **Write a note** with a few ideas.
5. **Turn a line into a task** — select text and press *Turn into task*.
6. **Add a file** — upload or drop one in.
7. **Write some code** — create a file such as `main.py`.
8. **Search** — open it from the sidebar or press `Ctrl/⌘ K`.
9. **Focus** — one task, one timer.

You can skip any step, go back, or close the tour. Replay it any time from **Settings → Workspace → Start tutorial** or the account menu (**Take the tour**).

## 4. Make it yours

- **Star** the projects you're working on to pin them in the sidebar.
- Pick **Light**, **Dark** or **System** in **Settings → Appearance** (mova follows your device by default).
- On desktop, turn on the optional **AI** helpers in **Settings → AI** ([how](AI.md)).
- On desktop, open **Files → Google Drive** to edit files that live in your Drive ([how](DRIVE.md)).

## Your account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to see which account you're using, your sync status, **Sync now**, **Settings**, **Take the tour** and **Sign out**.
