# Introduction

**mova** is a calm, project-based workspace. Tasks, notes, files, calendar, code and focus live together inside the projects you're working on, and the **Today** screen shows you what actually matters right now.

![The mova Today screen](screenshots/today.png)

## Where mova runs

| | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Desktop app** | macOS (Apple Silicon), Windows, Linux — [download](https://movadesktop.vercel.app/download) | Everyday use. Works offline, syncs through your Google Drive, edits Drive files, includes AI helpers |
| **Web app** | [movadesktopweb.vercel.app](https://movadesktopweb.vercel.app) | Using mova anywhere without installing anything |

Both use the same interface and the same Google sign-in. See [Web app](WEB.md) for exactly what differs.

## The ideas behind it

- **Project-first.** You don't think "I need to open my notes app." You think "I'm working on my robotics project" — so you open the project and everything is there.
- **Today, not everything.** You don't need to plan your whole life every morning. You need to know what matters today.
- **No productivity theatre.** Few buttons, few settings, few decisions.
- **AI that stays small.** It suggests; you decide and you do the work. mova works perfectly without it.
- **Your data stays yours.** On your device, and — if you choose — in your own Google Drive.
